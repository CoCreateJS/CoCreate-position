CoCreate Position

A small interactive guide to positioning, queries, and insertion references.

Move an element

Drag the panel by its header into the left or right snap area. The panel itself defines the allowed destinations with droppable-query.

Drag this panel
Its position can be controlled with CSS and the Position events. 
<div draggable droppable-query="$top .snap-area[]">...</div>

Declarative placement with position-query

The panel positions itself relative to the element selected by its query.

I’m positioned relative to the button. 
<button id="placement-anchor">Anchor</button>
<div position-query="$top #placement-anchor" position="bottom left">...</div>
